      The system-call boundary
      
        
      Objects call propose(),
      never speak().
      
        
      
          Traditional operating systems schedule scarce compute. A physical AI
          OS must also schedule something scarcer: a person’s attention.
        
      
        
      
          
      Gate
      Privacy before ranking.
      Identity, context, capability, audience, and expiry are hard constraints.
      
          
      Score
      Value minus interruption.
      Urgency and relevance compete with route fit, modality, and social cost.
      
          
      Route
      The object is part of the answer.
      A glow, haptic, or voice is selected only after a proposal earns attention.
